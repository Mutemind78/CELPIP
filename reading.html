📖 Reading Section

Master skimming, scanning, and deep-reading strategies to tackle all 4 parts of the CELPIP Reading module.

Part 1

Reading Correspondence

Read a personal or professional letter/email and answer comprehension questions. Practice tracking relationships and key messages.

Part 2

Reading to Apply a Diagram

Analyze visual information such as flyers, schedules, maps, or charts, and map them to textual descriptions or choices.

Part 3

Reading for Information

Read a factual passage split into multiple paragraphs. Learn to align specific statements with their corresponding paragraphs.

Part 4

Reading for Viewpoints

Read an editorial style debate or article exploring a specific viewpoint. Practice tracking abstract arguments and drawing logical conclusions.
