🎧 Listening Section

Develop effective listening strategies, understand spoken English in diverse contexts, and master the 6 Listening parts.

Part 1

Listening to Problem Solving

Listen to a conversation where individuals are discussing a problem and trying to find a resolution. Learn how to identify core issues and key arguments.

Part 2

Listening to a Daily Life Conversation

Understand interactions involving everyday activities, requests, and plans. Focus on details, tones, and informal expressions.

Part 3

Listening for Information

Listen to a factual presentation or interview. Learn to capture specific details, figures, and explanations.

Part 4

Listening to a News Item

Analyze a short broadcast reporting on a local or global event. Develop strategies to extract the who, what, when, and why.

Part 5

Listening to a Discussion

Listen to a group conversation or academic forum with multiple speakers. Master tracking viewpoints, agreements, and disagreements.

Part 6

Listening to Viewpoints

Analyze a complex commentary regarding a controversial issue. Focus on understanding advanced arguments, inferences, and perspectives.
