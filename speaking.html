🗣️ Speaking Section

Practice speaking clearly, structure answers with our formulas, and develop confidence for all 8 speaking tasks.

Task 1

Giving Advice

Provide helpful recommendations to a friend, family member, or colleague. Focus on polite structures, modal verbs, and practical arguments.

Task 2

Talking about a Personal Experience

Describe a memorable moment or event from your past. Practice using active past tenses, connectors, and descriptive descriptors.

Task 3

Describing a Scene

Look at an illustration and describe what is happening. Learn spatial vocabulary (foreground, background, next to) and active progressive tenses.

Task 4

Making Predictions

Look at the same illustration and predict what might happen next. Focus on future tense constructions and probability phrases.

Task 5

Comparing and Persuading

Compare two distinct choices and persuade a family member, friend, or coworker on why your preference is better. Practice comparative structures.

Task 6

Dealing with a Difficult Situation

Navigate an awkward or difficult situation (e.g. telling someone bad news, asking for a favor). Master empathetic, tactful tone control.

Task 7

Expressing Opinions

State your opinion on a general social interest topic. Leverage our O-R-E-R-E-C speaking formula to structure a fluent, professional response.

Task 8

Describing an Unusual Situation

Describe a unique, strange, or bizarre object or image to someone who cannot see it. Practice descriptive precision and spatial metaphors.
