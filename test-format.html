Test format

Know the format before you practise

Every CELPIP task has a fixed structure. Learn the parts, question counts and component times once, and you can spend your practice time on the language itself.

Official format information. The task names, question counts and component times on this page are published CELPIP format facts. The strategies, checklists and examples that accompany them are SelfPIP study guidance — not official CELPIP instruction, and no advice can promise a particular score. Always confirm current requirements at celpip.ca.

The test at a glance

One sitting, four ability areas. Use the sequence below as a map, then open the relevant SelfPIP section for task strategies and original practice.

  1. 1

    Listening

    Six parts · approximately 46–55 minutes

    Follow conversations, talks, news, discussions and viewpoints. Track what changes, what speakers mean and which detail supports each answer.

  2. 2

    Reading

    Four parts · approximately 43–56 minutes

    Read correspondence, apply a diagram, find information and compare viewpoints while moving between purpose, detail and evidence.

  3. 3

    Writing

    Two tasks · approximately 53 minutes

    Write one email and one survey response. There is no official per-task time limit, so practise completing both clearly and on topic.

  4. 4

    Speaking

    Eight tasks · 15 minutes

    Respond to eight short situations. Clear organisation, relevant language and a steady attempt matter more than complicated vocabulary.

Plan for the whole test. The component times explain the shape of the test, not a promise about question-by-question timing. Build accuracy first, then practise staying calm and moving on when a question is difficult.

Versions and score reporting

Two test versions

The CELPIP General Training test is available in two versions. They cover the same four ability areas, so prepare every task type rather than studying for one specific version.

Four CLB scores

Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking are reported separately on the Canadian Language Benchmark scale from CLB 1 to CLB 10. The organization you apply to decides which levels it requires.

Check current requirements before you register. SelfPIP is an independent study resource.
