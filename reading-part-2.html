Reading Part 2

Reading to Apply a Diagram

Use information from a visual source such as a schedule, flyer, map, chart, table, or comparison layout to answer questions embedded in a written context.

Section 1 — What Is This Part? Official format: 8 questions SelfPIP study guide
Golden rule: Question first → Locate the entry → Compare → Check every condition

What is this part?

In Part 2 the answers live partly outside the words: a visual source (a schedule, a table, a flyer, a map) holds the facts, and a short written context — often a message — describes what someone needs. You apply one to the other.

The task tests precise, selective reading: you must find the right row or section, compare it with the situation described, and respect every condition (times, prices, age limits, bookings).

Visual formats you may meet include:

  • schedule
  • event flyer
  • comparison table
  • menu
  • map
  • timetable
  • price chart
  • service information
  • program schedule
Official format vs SelfPIP advice: Part 2 officially contains 8 questions. The strategies, tables and examples on this page are SelfPIP study guidance — they are not official CELPIP rules, and the diagrams below are original SelfPIP creations.

Key Strategy

SelfPIP guidance

Read the question and the written context first — the diagram only matters once you know what you are looking for.

Read the question and context
↓
Identify what information is needed
↓
Locate it in the diagram
↓
Compare the relevant entries
↓
Choose the supported answer
Why this order works: the context tells you which columns matter (price? age? day?). Locating before comparing keeps you from re-reading the whole table for every option.
3

Important Skills

SelfPIP focus points

Eight skills cover almost every Part 2 question. Practise them one at a time.

1

Locate specific information

Find the exact cell, row or section.

Headings are your map — use them before reading anything else.

2

Compare entries

Which option fits best?

Compare only the rows the question allows.

3

Understand categories

Which column or group does this belong to?

Age groups, days and sections are the usual categories.

4

Follow dates and times

When does it start, end, or repeat?

Watch for a.m./p.m., ranges, and "closed on" notes.

5

Compare prices

Per class, per person, or total?

Check what the price covers before comparing numbers.

6

Understand conditions

Bookings, age limits, gear, weather.

A condition can quietly disqualify an option that looks perfect.

7

Apply information logically

Does the situation satisfy every rule?

One failed condition is enough to eliminate an option.

8

Recognize paraphrases

Same fact, different words.

"Under $10" can match "$8 per class" — the numbers decide.

Common Traps

SelfPIP guidance

⚠ Trap 1 — Reading the whole diagram equally

How it feels"I should study every cell so nothing surprises me."

InsteadRead the question, then jump straight to the rows and columns it needs.

⚠ Trap 2 — Ignoring the headings

How it feels"A price is a price, wherever it sits."

InsteadHeadings define what each number means — per class, per person, per week.

⚠ Trap 3 — Confusing similar entries

How it feels"Two rows look the same, so either answer works."

InsteadFind the one difference (day, age, fee) — it usually decides the question.

⚠ Trap 4 — Missing a restriction

How it feels"The time and price fit, so this is the answer."

InsteadCheck the condition column last: bookings, age limits and weather rules disqualify quietly.

⚠ Trap 5 — Comparing the wrong category

How it feels"$8 is cheaper than $60, so yoga wins."

InsteadCompare like with like: a course fee and a drop-in fee are different categories.

⚠ Trap 6 — Attractive but incomplete

How it feels"This option satisfies most of the message."

InsteadThe answer must satisfy every condition in the written context, not most of them.

Remember: these traps are SelfPIP study guidance, not official rules. Use them to review your own habits after practice.

Example A — Community Centre Schedule

Original practice

This schedule and email are original SelfPIP examples, not CELPIP material. Read the message first, then use the table to answer.

Riverbend Community Centre — Fall programs (fictional)
Activity Day Time Age group Fee Important condition
Morning Yoga Tuesday 7:00–8:00 a.m. 16+ $8 per class Bring your own mat
Youth Basketball Wednesday 4:00–5:30 p.m. 10–14 Free Gym shoes required
Pottery Basics Thursday 6:30–8:00 p.m. 18+ $60 for 6 weeks All materials included
Aqua Fit Saturday 9:00–10:00 a.m. 16+ $10 per class Book online at least 24 hours ahead
Saturday Craft Club Saturday 10:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m. Ages 5–9 (with an adult) $5 per child Maximum 12 children
From:
Maya Osei
To:
Riverbend Community Centre — front desk
Subject:
Which programs should we sign up for?

Hello,

I am signing up my daughter, Age 7, and I would like to join something myself. We can only come on Saturday mornings before noon, and I would like to keep the total under $10 for the two of us.

My neighbour Sam is 19 and works weekday evenings. He wants one early weekday class before work, also under $10, and he already owns a yoga mat.

Could you tell us which programs fit? Thanks so much!

Maya

Question 1 — Which activity should Maya choose for her daughter?

Question 2 — Which class fits Maya's neighbour Sam?

Question 3 — What must Aqua Fit participants remember?

Question 4 — Which statement about Pottery Basics is supported by the schedule?

What to notice: every tempting option quoted a real cell from the table. The supported answer is the one that satisfies all of the writer's conditions — day, time, age and price together.

Example B — Transportation Comparison

Original practice

This comparison table and message are original SelfPIP examples, not CELPIP material.

Harbour to Milltown — one-way travel options (fictional)
Option Price (one way) Travel time First departure Special features Restrictions
Coastal Train $24 55 min 6:40 a.m. Wi-Fi, quiet car No bicycles
Express Bus $15 1 h 40 min 5:30 a.m. Charging ports Peak departures sell out
Rental Car $49 + fuel 1 h 25 min Anytime Seats 4, roof rack Driver must be 21+
Harbour Ferry $18 2 h 10 min 8:00 a.m. Bike racks, café Cancelled in high winds
From:
Jordan
To:
Weekend trip group chat
Subject:
Milltown on Saturday — read before we vote

Hi all,

Quick facts before we choose how to get to Milltown on Saturday. We are four people plus two bikes. Nobody wants to spend more than $25 per person, and we must arrive before noon.

One more thing: the only one of us with a licence is Sami, who is 19.

Jordan

Question 1 — Which option is ruled out immediately because of the bikes?

Question 2 — Which option can the group NOT use, for a reason that has nothing to do with bikes?

Question 3 — Which option is the cheapest per person and still arrives before noon?

Question 4 — If the group chooses the ferry, what risk should they keep in mind?

What to notice: each question eliminated options for a different reason — bikes, age, price, weather. In Part 2, one failed condition is enough to remove an option, so check the restriction column even when the timing looks perfect.
