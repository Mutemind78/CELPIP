🟦 CARD 1 — Grammar: My Common Mistakes
MEMORIZE THE RIGHT VERSION
❌ suggest you to → ✅ suggest that you
❌ recommend you to → ✅ recommend that you
❌ interested to → ✅ interested in
❌ did not worked → ✅ did not work
❌ I have went → ✅ I have gone
❌ people are in a waiting → ✅ people are waiting
❌ since past 2 days → ✅ for the past 2 days
❌ a medical problems → ✅ medical problems
❌ one of my friend → ✅ one of my friends
❌ it gonna rain → ✅ it is going to rain
❌ I was good in maths → ✅ I was good at maths
❌ according to me → ✅ in my opinion / from my perspective
⭐ Rule: Don't make the sentence complicated if you're unsure. Simple + correct > complicated + incorrect.
🟩 CARD 2 — CELPIP Sentence Patterns
Learn these until they become automatic.
Giving an opinion
  • In my opinion, ...
  • From my perspective, ...
  • I strongly believe that ...
Giving reasons
  • One of the main reasons is that...
  • Another important factor is...
  • The main reason for this is...
Giving examples
  • For example, ...
  • For instance, ...
  • A good example of this would be...
Explaining results
  • As a result, ...
  • This would allow people to...
  • This could lead to...
  • In the long run, ...
Comparing
  • Compared with..., ...
  • On the other hand, ...
  • While X has some advantages, I believe Y would be better because...

🟧 CARD 4 — Vocabulary: Understand, Don't Force
Useful for Reading/Listening, but don't try to force them into every Speaking answer.
  • Deceased → dead; formal
  • Adherent → person who strongly supports an idea/belief
  • Aghast → extremely shocked
  • Attire → clothing
  • Genre → category/type of music, film, literature
  • Quirk → unusual characteristic/habit
  • Astonished → extremely surprised
⭐ Remember: Understand → recognize → use naturally.
You don't get extra CELPIP points simply for throwing difficult words into your answer.
🟥 CARD 5 — Low Priority
Don't waste your limited study time actively memorizing these.
  • Crony → close friend/associate, often with a negative implication
  • Cadaver → dead human body, usually medical/scientific context
  • Convener → person who organizes/calls a meeting
  • Drool → saliva unintentionally coming from the mouth
  • Starving → extremely hungry

Know them if you encounter them, but move on.

🟪 CARD 6 — Speaking Formula
When CELPIP gives you a Speaking question:
O → R → E → R → E → C

Opinion • Reason 1 • Example 1 • Reason 2 • Example 2 • Conclusion

Example Question: Should the city improve public transportation?

  • Opinion: From my perspective, the city should improve public transportation.
  • Reason 1: One of the main reasons is affordability.
  • Example: For example, students and low-income workers may not be able to afford a car.
  • Reason 2: Another important factor is traffic congestion.
  • Example: Better public transportation could reduce the number of cars on the road.
  • Conclusion: Therefore, I believe improving public transportation would be a better long-term solution.
🔥 Your rule: Don't search for fancy words while speaking. Get the sentence out correctly and continuously.
🟫 CARD 7 — English Brain
This is your warehouse card. When you're standing around waiting, look around and create English sentences:
See a pallet? The pallet has been delivered to the production line.
Someone is waiting? The operator is waiting for the remaining material.
Something is delayed? There appears to be a delay because the required material hasn't arrived.
Someone moves material? The material needs to be moved to the designated area.
Something is finished? The required material has already been delivered.
Something might happen? The production line may experience a delay if the material doesn't arrive on time.
🟫 CARD 8 — 5-Minute Grammar Drill
Take one sentence and change the tense. Do this with 5 different sentences every day.
Present: I work in a warehouse.
Past: I worked in a warehouse.
Future: I will work in a warehouse.
Present perfect: I have worked in a warehouse.
With reason: I work in a warehouse because I want to gain practical experience.
With result: I work long hours; as a result, I have limited time to study.
🟢 CARD 9 — Work & Free-Time Study Routine
How to integrate CELPIP practice into your job and everyday downtime without burnout.
Time / Phase Actionable Strategy
During Work Shifts
(Micro-learning)		 • Apply Card 7 (English Brain): Whenever you notice a delay, a moving pallet, or someone waiting, silently construct full English sentences.
• Mental Tense Shifting: Take a simple work action (e.g., "I check inventory") and cycle it through past, future, and perfect tenses in your head.
Breaks & Downtime
(5-Minute Drills)		 • Grammar Drill (Card 8): Pick 1 sentence during your coffee break and write or speak it in 6 different structures.

(Active Practice)		 • Speaking Formula Drill (Card 6): Pick a random topic (e.g., public transport, workplace tech) and record yourself answering using the O-R-E-R-E-C formula for 60 seconds.
• Active Vocabulary (Card 3): Pick 2 words and write 1 sentence for each.
