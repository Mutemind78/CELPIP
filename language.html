Language Connection Map

Grammar × Vocabulary

Precise words need correct machinery around them: an honest opinion, two deadlines, a verb that matches its subject.

🔗

Connectors × Grammar

Every connector owns a grammar pattern: although + clause, despite + noun, therefore + full sentence. Wrong pattern = lost clarity.

🗣️

A word only counts as active once you can say it clearly. English stress carries meaning — a misplaced beat can change the word.

8 SelfPIP Language Rules

1

One idea, one sentence. If you need three commas and a second “and”, split it in two.

2

The subject decides the verb. Find the true subject and ignore the words between it and the verb.

3

Finished time takes past simple (yesterday, last year); unfinished time takes present perfect (so far, this week).

4

Singular countable nouns never stand alone — they need a, an, the, or a plural.

5

No word without a partner. Learn the collocation with the word: depend on, not depend of.

6

Every connector owns a pattern: although + clause, despite + noun, therefore + full sentence.

7

Stress carries meaning. Hit the key content word in each sentence and let the small words fall away.

8

Clarity beats complexity. The rater must catch your idea on the first read or first listen.

The 10-Minute Language Routine

Minutes 1–3

Grammar rep

Open one accordion topic, then write two corrected sentences using that rule.

Minutes 4–6

Word bank

Pick three words from a bank, write one sentence each, and press 🔊 to hear them.

Minutes 7–8

Connectors

Rebuild two flat sentences with however, although, or despite — check the pattern.

Minutes 9–10

Hear → repeat → record

Say one paragraph aloud, record it once, and compare with the model voice.

Ten honest minutes a day beats a two-hour cram the night before — the routine keeps all four skills alive between full practice tests.
